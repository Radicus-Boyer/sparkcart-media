FILE: 110 The Evil Guest 666 (58s).mp4
YT TITLE: The "Evil Guest" Every Roblox Kid Feared 👻 #Shorts
YT DESCRIPTION: Guests arrived in September 2008 so you could try Roblox without an account, but they couldn't chat with friends, buy items or save progress. The legend of Guest 666, "An Evil Guest" with admin powers, was a hoax: guest numbers ran 0 to 9999 and players edited their browser cookies to become 666. Roblox removed guests in October 2017, and the last workarounds were gone by November 2018.
