FILE: 46 - Club Penguin (59s).mp4
YT TITLE: Club Penguin Was Built by 3 Dads 🐧 #Shorts
YT DESCRIPTION: Three dads, Lance Priebe, Lane Merrifield and Dave Krysko, couldn't find a safe site for their kids, so they built one. Club Penguin opened in October 2005 with 15,000 users, sold to Disney for about $350 million less than two years later, and passed 200 million accounts by 2013, with no ads and roughly 70 of 100 staff working on safety. It closed on March 30, 2017 (the fastest ban that day took under 40 seconds), and the fan copy Club Penguin Rewritten was shut down by police in 2022 after a Disney request.

YT TITLE: Angry Birds Was Rovio's Last Shot 🐦‍🔥 #Shorts
YT DESCRIPTION: Rovio had already made 51 games for other companies and could afford just one more of its own. Jaakko Iisalo's grumpy birds won before there was any gameplay, the slingshot was nearly rejected as too obvious, and the pigs came from his childhood drawings, not swine flu. FILE: 74 ET Burial (57s).mp4
YT TITLE: Atari Buried Its Games in the Desert 🏜️ #Shorts
YT DESCRIPTION: E.T. for the Atari 2600 was made in just five weeks; Atari produced 5 million cartridges but sold about 1.5 million at first. In September 1983 truckloads of Atari games were dumped at the Alamogordo, New Mexico landfill and covered in concrete. A 2014 dig found about 1,300 cartridges from 59 games (Atari's records said roughly 700,000 were buried), one E.T. sold for over $1,500 and another went to the Smithsonian.
