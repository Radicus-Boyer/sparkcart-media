FILE: 48 The Roblox Oof (70s).mp4
YT TITLE: The Roblox OOF Was Never Roblox's Sound. Here's the Story 💀 #Shorts
YT DESCRIPTION: The Roblox "oof" came from Messiah, a PC game released in 2000. The sound is about a third of a second long. Roblox used it as its death sound from 2006, it became a huge meme, and in 2019 composer Tommy Tallarico claimed ownership. After a deal, it was removed in July 2022 over a licensing issue, and Roblox brought it back in July 2025.
