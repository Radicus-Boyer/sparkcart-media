FILE: 90 Final Fantasy (55s).mp4
YT TITLE: Why Is It Called "Final" Fantasy? 💎 #Shorts
YT DESCRIPTION: Square's Final Fantasy (directed by Hironobu Sakaguchi) began life as Fighting Fantasy, until that name turned out to belong to a gamebook series. Composer Nobuo Uematsu said "Final" meant Sakaguchi would quit if it flopped, with Square facing possible bankruptcy; Sakaguchi later said any F word would have done. Four heroes, six classes, a secret sliding puzzle added by programmer Nasir Gebelli, 520,000 copies shipped in Japan at first and about 1.3 million sold worldwide by 1994.
